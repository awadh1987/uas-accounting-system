## 2. Completely Missing Modules (Zero Real Implementation)

1. **Fixed Assets Management Module**: No asset register, categories, acquisition capitalization, depreciation schedules/methods, automated monthly depreciation runs, asset transfers, disposals, revaluations, or impairment.
2. **Financial Consolidation & Intercompany Module**: No parent/subsidiary structure, intercompany accounts/eliminations, group COA mapping, or multi-company consolidation engine.
3. **Tax, VAT & Withholding Tax Module**: No configurable tax codes/rates/effective dates, automated output VAT / input VAT / WHT ledger splitting, tax reconciliation, or tax return reporting.
4. **Cheques & Commercial Paper Module**: No cheque inventory, received/issued cheque statuses, clearing dates, bounced cheque reversals, or Post-Dated Cheques (PDC).
5. **Comprehensive Budgeting & Budget Control Module**: No account-level annual/monthly/departmental budgets, budget revisions, or hard/soft expenditure blocking controls.
